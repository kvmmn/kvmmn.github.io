      Project-risk detection in Jira

      Production ML and AI project management, engineering firm

      10+ days → <24 hto detect a project risk

      Problem
      Risks were found by manual review, often 10 or more days after the first signal.
      Built
      An NLP and gradient-boosting pipeline that scores risk from unstructured project data and raises explained alerts inside Jira, where the team already works.
      Result
      Risks flagged in under 24 hours, with about 80% less manual review. One of five AI systems I shipped there, alongside finance intake, scheduling, pricing, and procurement.

    2. 02

      Demand forecasting in production

      Data science and MLOps, retail and food service (SnappStore)

      ~12%fewer stock-outs

      Problem
      Replenishment needed reliable weekly demand numbers, inside the tools teams already used.
      Built
      Hybrid ARIMA and LSTM forecasting, served as a FastAPI service on Docker and Kubernetes and wired into CRM, order, and POS workflows, with dashboards for operations.
      Result
      About 12% fewer stock-outs, with forecast error of 10 to 15% MAPE.

      Python · ARIMA · LSTM · FastAPI · Docker · Kubernetes · Metabase

    Questions

    Before you book.

    What does it cost?

    The sprint is a fixed scope at a fixed price. I quote it after the fit call, once the workflow and its complexity are clear. Workshops are priced by format and group size.

    What do you need from us?

    One person who owns the workflow, time with the people who run it today, and access to the data or documents it uses.

    Do you also build the system?

    Sometimes. After the blueprint, a scoped build or advisory is possible and priced separately. Many teams build it themselves, and the blueprint is written for that.

    Remote or on site?

    Berlin based and remote friendly. On site in Berlin when it helps the work.

    What if a sprint is the wrong tool?

    Then I will say so on the fit call, before you pay anything. Sometimes the right first step is a workshop, or a smaller fix.
